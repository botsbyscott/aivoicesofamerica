AI Voices of America

A study on how Americans perceive AI-generated voices.

About the project

AI Voices of America is a research project investigating how listeners perceive synthetic voices, and how those perceptions vary across speakers, regions, and demographics. Participants contribute short voice recordings that help us study the boundaries between human and AI-generated speech.

The study is conducted in partnership with the University of Georgia and follows IRB-approved protocols for voice data collection and storage.
